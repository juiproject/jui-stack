Site configuration for the help system. Everything above the second `---` is
front matter and is read by the build; this body text is ignored.

- **title** — shown in the header and browser tab.
- **tagline** — the line under the hero heading.
- **categoryOrder** — the left-to-right / top-to-bottom order of categories (by folder name).
- **popular** — article slugs featured on the landing page.
- **backLabel / backUrl** — the top-right link back to your app. Leave `backUrl` empty to hide it.
