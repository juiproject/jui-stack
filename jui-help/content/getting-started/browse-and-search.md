There are three ways to get to an article.

## Browse by category

The home page lists every category as a card with its article count. Open one to
see all of its articles in order.

## Search

Type in the search box on the home page. Results update as you type and match an
article's title, description and category. Searching is entirely client-side —
nothing leaves the page.

## Read

An article shows a **table of contents** built from its section headings; the
current section is highlighted as you scroll. At the foot of each article you
will find *related* articles to continue with.
