Welcome! This is a **portable, standalone help system**. Everything you are
reading is authored as a markdown file with a little front matter for metadata,
and rendered entirely in the browser — there is no server behind it.

## How it is organised

Content is grouped into **categories** (the cards on the home page), and each
category holds **articles** like this one. You can:

- **Search** from the box on the home page — it matches titles, descriptions and categories.
- **Browse** by opening a category to see all of its articles.
- **Read** an article, follow its table of contents, and jump to *related* articles.

> This is sample content shipped with the system. Replace the `content/` folder
> with your own articles and rebuild — see *Add or edit an article*.

## What powers it

The whole thing is four small static files plus your content. It runs from a
plain file server, or even straight off disk. When you are ready to make it your
own, start with *Add or edit an article* and *Theming and styles*.
